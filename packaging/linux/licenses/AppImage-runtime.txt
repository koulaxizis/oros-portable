AppImage runtime (Linux .AppImage only)
=======================================

The first part of orOS-<version>-x86_64.AppImage is the AppImage type 2
runtime, which mounts the rest of the file and starts orOS. It was added by
appimagetool 1.9.0 when the AppImage was built.

                  https://github.com/AppImage/type2-runtime

The runtime is linked statically with:

  libfuse 3.15.0  LGPL-2.1 (the library), Miklos Szeredi and contributors,
                  with the AppImage project's patch to mount.c
                  https://github.com/libfuse/libfuse
                  The licence text is in LGPL-2.1.txt next to this file.
  squashfuse 0.5.2  BSD-2-Clause, Dave Vasilevsky and contributors
                  https://github.com/vasi/squashfuse
  zstd            BSD-3-Clause, Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates
                  https://github.com/facebook/zstd
  zlib            zlib licence, Jean-loup Gailly and Mark Adler
                  https://zlib.net/
  mimalloc        MIT, Microsoft Corporation, Daan Leijen
                  https://github.com/microsoft/mimalloc
  musl libc       MIT, Rich Felker et al.
                  https://musl.libc.org/

The runtime's complete source and build scripts, including the exact library
versions and patches, are in the type2-runtime repository above (see
scripts/common/install-dependencies.sh and patches/). Under the LGPL you may
replace libfuse with a modified version: rebuild the runtime from that source
with your libfuse, then put it in front of the AppImage's squashfs payload
(appimagetool --runtime-file). WRITTEN-OFFER.txt also covers this part.
